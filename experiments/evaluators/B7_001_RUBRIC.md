# B7-001 Evaluator Rubric — Multi-Agent Research Synthesis

Version: 0.1

## Question under evaluation

Assess whether a provider-independent Runtime Intelligence layer has a plausible defensible role above existing AI observability, evaluation and gateway products. Cover: (A) observability/evaluation, (B) model/provider gateways, (C) agent governance, and (D) the remaining cross-resource optimisation hypothesis.

## Scoring

Total: 100 points.

### A. Observability / evaluation landscape — 20

Full credit requires a fair account of the capabilities already provided by major observability/evaluation products and avoids describing tracing/evals as an unoccupied market.

### B. Gateway / routing landscape — 20

Full credit requires recognition that model/provider routing, fallbacks, budgets, reliability and some policy controls already exist.

### C. Agent governance landscape — 20

Full credit requires recognition that agent/tool traces, registries, budgets, permissions and governance are already developing quickly and therefore are not sufficient differentiation alone.

### D. Remaining cross-resource hypothesis — 25

Full credit requires a precise, narrow remaining hypothesis involving some combination of:
- task resource profiling;
- context/instruction compilation;
- agent spawn economics;
- cross-resource marginal compute utility;
- useful-state-change / loop detection;
- verification allocation;
- outcome-conditioned execution-policy learning.

### E. Epistemic and commercial discipline — 15

Full credit requires:
- facts separated from project hypotheses;
- at least one credible reason the commercial thesis could fail;
- no assertion of uniqueness without evidence;
- competitor capabilities not understated to manufacture differentiation.

## Multi-agent-specific analysis

For experiments comparing parent-only vs multi-agent variants, separately record:
- number of agents/subagents;
- distinct subquestions assigned;
- overlapping evidence/source use;
- unique facts or analysis contributed by each agent;
- coordination/synthesis overhead;
- whether the additional agents changed the final score/outcome.

Do not infer causal value from agent existence alone.

## Failure conditions

The run fails regardless of numerical score if:
- it materially misrepresents known competitor capabilities;
- it treats generic observability/routing as the validated unique wedge;
- it omits a substantive failure/risk case;
- it presents an unvalidated project hypothesis as established market fact.

## Suggested success threshold

85/100 with no failure condition.
