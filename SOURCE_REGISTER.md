# Source Register

The detailed research source register is maintained at:

- `research/SOURCE_REGISTER.md`

This root file exists to satisfy project bootstrap/continuity conventions while avoiding duplicate source lists.

## Rules

- Prefer primary provider documentation, original papers, official repositories, standards bodies, and direct benchmarks.
- Distinguish provider claims, peer-reviewed findings, preprints, third-party commentary, community observations, and our own experiments.
- Record retrieval/publication dates and model/provider versions when materially relevant.
- Do not treat a benchmark or vendor claim as universal evidence without task-specific validation.
